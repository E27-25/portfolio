// PROFILE

About

UPDATED · NOV 2026

Research and AI engineer who likes turning ideas from papers into systems people can actually use.

My research is about efficient and reliable LLMs for Thai: quantization, speculative decoding, KV-cache management, inference energy and calibration. On the applied side I build agentic workflows, retrieval-augmented generation and knowledge graphs for tourism, finance, healthcare, government, accessibility, education and industry.

Right now I'm building equipment-health monitoring and an LLM-powered maintenance-report generator at Asia Energy Tech, and working on state-matched quantization for LLMs with compressed KV caches. In 2026 I had ten papers accepted at international conferences, eight as first author.

I also love teaching: I've been a TA for KMITL, the Digital Government Development Agency (DGA) and AIAT's Super AI Engineer program.
